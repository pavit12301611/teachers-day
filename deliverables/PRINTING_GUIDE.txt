TEACHERS' DAY FOLD CARDS — PRINTING GUIDE
==========================================

What is included
----------------
• Teachers_Day_Fold_Cards_83_Teachers.odt
• Exactly 83 individual, name-specific cards — one for every person in staff.csv
• Every card has a clean personal note, hand-drawn watercolour portrait, and a QR code
  that opens that teacher's own tribute page at teachers-day-rosy.vercel.app.
• The layout is vertical A4 with two fold-and-give cards on each page. Since there are
  83 teachers, the final (42nd) sheet has Gabrial's card only; no extra filler card
  has been added.

How to print
------------
1. Open the ODT in LibreOffice Writer (recommended) or another ODT-compatible editor.
2. Choose A4 paper, Portrait orientation, and Colour printing.
3. Set scaling to 100% / Actual Size. Do not choose “Fit to page”.
4. Print single-sided. The artwork, message, and QR code are all on that side.
5. On the first 41 sheets, cut on the faint horizontal dashed line between the cards.
6. Fold each cut-out card on its vertical dotted line with the portrait and
   “HAPPY TEACHERS' DAY” cover on the outside. The personal message and QR are inside.

Quick QR check
--------------
The companion file Teachers_Day_Card_QR_Links.txt lists every encoded destination.
You can scan a printed sample before printing the full set if you want to check your
printer's contrast. The QR blocks are vector artwork, so they stay sharp at 100% size.
