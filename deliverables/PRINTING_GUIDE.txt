TEACHERS' DAY FOLD CARDS — PRINTING GUIDE
==========================================

What is included
----------------
• Teachers_Day_Fold_Cards_83_Teachers.odt
• 83 individual cards, one for every person in staff.csv
• A final spare St. Mary's Family card so the 42nd sheet is also two-up
• Every named card has that teacher's watercolour portrait and a QR code that opens
  their own tribute page at teachers-day-rosy.vercel.app.

How to print
------------
1. Open the ODT in LibreOffice Writer (recommended) or another ODT-compatible editor.
2. Choose A4 paper, Portrait orientation, and Colour printing.
3. Set scaling to 100% / Actual Size. Do not choose “Fit to page”.
4. Print single-sided. The artwork, note, and QR code are all on that side.
5. Use the faint horizontal dashed line between the two cards as the cut line.
6. For each cut-out card, fold on its vertical dotted line with the portrait and
   “HAPPY TEACHERS' DAY” cover on the outside. The personal note and QR sit inside.

Card order
----------
Each page has the next two staff members in the same order as staff.csv. The last
sheet pairs Gabrial with the spare St. Mary's Family card.

Quick QR check
--------------
The companion file Teachers_Day_Card_QR_Links.txt lists every encoded destination.
You can scan a printed sample before printing the full set if you want to check your
printer's contrast. The QR blocks are vector artwork, so they stay sharp at 100% size.
