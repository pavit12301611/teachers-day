TEACHERS' DAY CARDS — PRINTING GUIDE
=====================================

What is included
----------------
• Teachers_Day_Printable_Cards_83_Teachers.odt
• Exactly 83 individual, name-specific cards — one for every person in staff.csv.
• Each card has a clean front with the teacher's embedded hand-drawn watercolour
  portrait, name, designation, and HAPPY TEACHERS' DAY greeting.
• The reverse has that teacher's large QR code and a short, personal thank-you note.
• All portraits and QR artwork are embedded inside the ODT itself. Nothing needs to be
  downloaded when you open or print the file.

Document order
--------------
The ODT contains 84 ordered sides (42 physical A4 sheets):
  Sheet 1 FRONT: teachers 1 and 2
  Sheet 1 BACK:  QR backs for teachers 1 and 2
  Sheet 2 FRONT, Sheet 2 BACK, and so on.

There are two landscape cards stacked vertically on every A4 side. The final sheet has
Gabrial's front and back at the top only, because there are exactly 83 teachers.

How to print
------------
1. Open the ODT in LibreOffice Writer (recommended).
2. Select A4 paper, Portrait orientation, and Colour printing.
3. Select two-sided / duplex printing and choose “Flip on long edge”.
4. Set scaling to 100% / Actual Size. Do not choose “Fit to page”.
5. Print sheet 1 first as a quick alignment test: front portrait on one side, large QR
   back on the other. Then print the remaining sheets.
6. Cut each sheet on the faint horizontal dashed line. No fold is needed: each piece is
   a proper double-sided card ready to hand to its teacher.

If your printer has no duplex mode, print each FRONT/BACK pair manually on the same
sheet (keeping the paper orientation unchanged). The small lower-margin labels identify
the sheet number and side.

Quick QR check
--------------
The companion file Teachers_Day_Card_QR_Links.txt lists all 83 encoded destinations.
The QR artwork is vector-based and intentionally large, so it stays sharp and easy to
scan after printing.
