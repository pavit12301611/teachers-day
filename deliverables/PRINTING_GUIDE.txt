TEACHERS' DAY FOLDED CARDS — PRINTING GUIDE
============================================

What is included
----------------
• Teachers_Day_Folded_Cards_83_Teachers.odt
• Exactly 83 individual, name-specific folded cards — one for every person in staff.csv.
• Every card has three personal parts:
  1. Cover: embedded hand-drawn watercolour portrait, name, designation, and HAPPY
     TEACHERS' DAY greeting.
  2. Back cover: a large QR code with a short, impactful thank-you line.
  3. Inside: a personal note for that teacher and a simple closing message.
• All portraits and QR artwork are embedded inside the ODT itself. Nothing needs to be
  downloaded when you open or print the file.

Document order
--------------
The ODT contains 84 ordered sides (42 physical A4 sheets):
  Sheet 1 OUTSIDE: QR back cover on the left + portrait front cover on the right.
  Sheet 1 INSIDE:  matching personal note on the reverse side.
  Sheet 2 OUTSIDE, Sheet 2 INSIDE, and so on.

Two folded cards are stacked vertically on each A4 side. The final sheet has Gabrial's
card at the top only, because there are exactly 83 teachers.

How to print and finish
-----------------------
1. Open the ODT in LibreOffice Writer (recommended).
2. Select A4 paper, Portrait orientation, and Colour printing.
3. Select two-sided / duplex printing and choose “Flip on long edge”.
4. Set scaling to 100% / Actual Size. Do not choose “Fit to page”.
5. Print sheet 1 first as a test. The portrait cover must be on one side, and its
   matching personal note must be on the reverse.
6. Cut each sheet on the faint horizontal dashed line.
7. Fold each cut-out card on its vertical dotted line, with the portrait and
   HAPPY TEACHERS' DAY cover facing out. The QR will be on the back and the personal
   note will be inside.

If your printer has no duplex mode, print each OUTSIDE/INSIDE pair manually on the
same sheet, keeping the paper orientation unchanged. Small lower-margin labels identify
the matching sheet number and side.

Quick QR check
--------------
The companion file Teachers_Day_Card_QR_Links.txt lists all 83 encoded destinations.
The QR artwork is vector-based and intentionally large, so it stays sharp and easy to
scan after printing.
